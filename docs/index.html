Hop

Numbered YouTube live channels for LG webOS TVs

Hop puts the live channels you watch on a simple numbered grid. Press a number on the remote and that channel starts playing in the real YouTube app, with your own login, your Premium, and no ads. It feels like changing channels on a normal TV.

Install Hop

What you see

Three steps, no new player

1Press a numberHop shows your channels as tiles 1 to 15. Press one or two digits, or move with the arrows and press OK.
2Hop finds the streamA small service on the TV asks YouTube which of the channel's streams is live right now and picks the main one.
3YouTube plays itHop hands the stream to the TV's own YouTube app. Your login, Premium and settings are all yours.

Number keys that behave like a TV

With channels 1 to 15, a single 1 is ambiguous: it could be channel 1 or the start of 10 to 15. Hop handles this the way a set-top box does.

You pressHop does
7Opens channel 7 at once. No channel starts with 7, so there is nothing to wait for.
1Shows "1 · Bhakthi TV" and waits 1.5 seconds for a second digit, then opens channel 1.
1 2Opens channel 12 at once.
1 9Shows "No channel 19" and clears.
OK while typingOpens the typed number immediately.
Back while typingCancels the entry. A second Back exits Hop.

Always the main stream

During festivals and big news days a channel may run five or ten live streams at once. The channel's ordinary "live" link then points at the newest event stream, not the regular 24/7 feed. Hop looks at all of a channel's live streams and picks the one most people are watching, which is almost always the main feed. For the rare exception you can give a channel a prefer hint, a few words from the title of the stream you want.

Switching channels from inside YouTube

Once YouTube is playing, its Back key goes to YouTube's own home, not back to Hop. The fix is built into the TV: Quick Access binds any app to a long press of a number key, and it works from inside any other app.

1Hold 0Press and hold the 0 key for about two seconds. The Quick Access screen opens.
2Pick a slotChoose a free slot from 1 to 8 and select Hop.
3Hop, then hopWhile YouTube plays, hold that key: Hop comes back. Press a channel number. Done.

Hop ignores held keys, so the long press never types a digit by mistake.

Small by design

Installed sizeAbout 200 KB, including the channel logos.
Runs onLG webOS 6 and later. Tested on webOS 24.
NetworkOnly youtube.com, to look up live streams. Nothing else, ever.
PermissionsOne: launching another app, so it can start YouTube.
AccountsNone. Hop has no login and keeps no data.

Get it

Hop is open source under the MIT licence. Installing it needs LG's Developer Mode, a free LG developer account and about twenty minutes the first time. The step-by-step guide is in the repository.
